Shows the difference in mean LLM calls between the Calls and Battery + Shelf objectives. Negative values indicate fewer calls under the Calls objective. supplementary_battery_productivity.png
Shows the descriptive battery-aware productivity score for each model and objective. The objective producing the highest score differed between models, so the Battery objective did not produce a consistent improvement.

INTERPRETATION NOTES

Deliveries per 1000 LLM calls measures deliveries relative to model-interaction frequency. It does not measure latency, token usage, GPU time, energy consumption, or computational cost.

The natural-language and JSON results compare the complete prompt configurations, including their response contracts and parsing paths. They do not isolate input representation alone.

Battery-aware productivity is a descriptive combination of deliveries and charging-station utilisation. It is not a measure of optimal battery management.
